# Chapter 22: Introduction to VLA Models

This chapter explains Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models in very simple words. You will learn what they are, how they are different from traditional robots, and why they help create general-purpose robots. You will also see how VLAs developed over time (RT-1, RT-2, and more) and learn about open-source projects like OpenVLA and today’s latest models.

## What are Vision-Language-Action (VLA) Models?

VLA models are a new type of robotics system that combines **vision**, **language**, and **action**.
This means these models can:

* understand what they **see**
* understand what humans **say**
* decide what **action** the robot should take

Traditional robots follow fixed programming. But VLA models work more like humans. They can understand instructions in natural language and use visual information around them to perform many different tasks.

## Traditional Robotics vs. VLA-Powered Systems

### Traditional Robotics

* **Specialized Tasks**: Made for one specific job, usually repeated again and again.
* **Explicit Programming**: Needs new coding for every new task.
* **Limited Generalization**: Cannot easily handle new or unexpected situations.
* **Perception-Action Loops**: Uses separate “seeing” and “doing” modules with limited smart reasoning.

### VLA-Powered Systems

* **General-Purpose Capabilities**: Can perform many different tasks in changing environments.
* **Natural Language Understanding**: Understands human instructions in simple language.
* **Visual Context Integration**: Uses visual information to understand the surroundings.
* **Adaptive & Robust**: Learns from different data and can adjust to new or unseen situations.

## Historical Evolution of VLAs

VLA models have improved quickly because of advances in deep learning and large language models:

* **RT-1 (Robotics Transformer 1)**: One of the first models showing that transformers can control robots using real-world data. It helped robots learn many general skills.
* **RT-2 (Robotics Transformer 2)**: Improved RT-1 by using multimodal LLMs. It allows robots to understand language instructions better and use visual reasoning for actions.
* **Beyond RT-2**: New research is focused on making VLAs faster, safer, more general, and better at complex thinking.

## Open-Source Initiatives and State-of-the-Art

Open-source projects are helping VLA research grow faster:

* **OpenVLA**: A community project offering open-source VLA models and tools. It helps more people learn and build VLA systems.
* **Current State-of-the-Art**: Modern VLAs have strong visual understanding, better grounding of language to actions, and safer, more precise physical control. These models are trained on large and diverse datasets, making them strong and reliable in real-world settings.
