# Quickstart: Humanoid Robot Development (Part 8)

Welcome to **Part 8: Humanoid Robot Development**! In this section, you will learn the basics of designing, controlling, and interacting with humanoid robots—robots that look and move like humans. Whether you are a student, researcher, or hobbyist, this guide will give you the essential knowledge to get started in this exciting field.

## What You Will Learn

In this part, you will explore:

* **Humanoid Anatomy**: Learn how robot bodies are inspired by humans. Understand the upper and lower body structure, sensor-equipped heads, and special joints that allow movement.
* **Locomotion and Balance**: Discover how robots walk on two legs. Learn about **Center of Mass (CoM)**, **Zero Moment Point (ZMP)** theory, and walking controllers that help robots move safely on different terrains and perform actions like running or jumping.
* **Manipulation and Dexterity**: Understand how robot arms and hands are designed for both strong and delicate grasping. Learn about fine motor control, tool use, and complex object manipulation.
* **Whole-Body Control**: Explore advanced techniques that control the robot’s entire body. Learn about inverse kinematics, prioritizing tasks, maintaining balance, and responding safely to external forces.
* **Natural Human-Robot Interaction (HRI)**: Learn how to design robots that interact naturally and safely with humans. This includes human-like appearance and movements, gestures, eye contact, personal space awareness, and safety mechanisms.
