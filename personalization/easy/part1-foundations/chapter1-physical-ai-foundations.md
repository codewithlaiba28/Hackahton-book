# Chapter 1: Physical AI and Embodied Intelligence

## What is Physical AI?

Physical AI is artificial intelligence with a real body that learns by doing things in the real world.

**Physical AI** = Has a body + learns by doing + touches the real world

**Regular AI** = Only works on computers + no body + learns from data

Physical AI is smarter because it actually experiences the world.

## Six Main Principles

Physical AI needs these six things:

1. **Body** - A real physical form to interact with the world
2. **Seeing and Feeling** - Sensors to understand what's around it
3. **Moving** - Ability to move and act in the world
4. **Learning** - Gets better from experience
5. **Making Choices** - Can decide what to do without human help
6. **Understanding Situations** - Adapts behavior based on what's happening

## Why Humanoid Robots?

Robots shaped like humans are best because:
- They fit into human spaces (stairs, doors, tools)
- People trust and understand them better
- One robot can do many different jobs

## Real-World Uses Today

- **Factories** - Robots do dangerous and repetitive work
- **Hospitals** - Surgery robots and prosthetic limbs
- **Delivery** - Self-driving cars and delivery robots
- **Working with Humans** - Robots that understand gestures and emotions

## Future and Challenges

**Future**: Better sensors, robots working more with humans, personal robots, exploring dangerous places

**Challenges**: Ethics, safety, power, keeping robots working reliably
