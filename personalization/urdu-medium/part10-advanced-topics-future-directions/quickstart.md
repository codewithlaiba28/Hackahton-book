## **What You Will Learn**

Is part mein, hum un tamam aspects ka jaiza lete hain jo Physical AI ke frontier ko define karte hain:

* **Advanced Perception Systems**: Robotics ke liye 3D computer vision ke intricacies discover karein, semantic aur instance segmentation, real-time object tracking, scene flow, sensor fusion, aur emerging sensor technologies ka latest.
* **Robot Learning and Adaptation**: Explore karein ke robots kaise meta-learning, few-shot learning, online adaptation, human-in-the-loop learning, learning from demonstrations, transfer learning, aur generalization aur robustness achieve karne ke strategies ke zariye learn aur adapt karte hain.
* **Distributed Robotics and Fleet Management**: Samjhein ke multiple robots ko coordinate karna, fleet communication networks, task allocation, swarm robotics, decentralized decision-making, aur resource management efficient operations ke liye kitna complex hai.
* **Ethical Considerations and Safety**: Examine karein robot ethics, societal impact, safety standards aur regulations, accountability, transparency, privacy, data security, bias detection aur mitigation, aur robotic systems ki long-term sustainability.
* **The Future of Physical AI**: Emerging technologies in robotics, quantum computing ke potential applications, brain-computer interfaces, autonomous decision-making, humanoid robots ka evolving role society mein, aur open research frontiers jo future innovators ke liye beckon karte hain, ko dekhein.
