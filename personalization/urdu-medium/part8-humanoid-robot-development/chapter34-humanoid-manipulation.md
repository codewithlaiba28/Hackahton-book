# **Chapter 34: Humanoid Manipulation**

Humanoid robots ke arms aur hands human limbs ki tarah design kiye gaye hain. Yeh unhein **grasp, hold, aur manipulate objects** mein dexterity aur precision provide karte hain.

---

## **Design of Arms and Hands**

### **Arms**

* Robot arms mein multiple joints hote hain jo human arms ki tarah move karte hain:

  * **Shoulder:** Up/down (pitch), rotate (yaw), aur tilt (roll) kar sakta hai
  * **Elbow:** Human elbow ki tarah bend karta hai (pitch)
  * **Wrist:** Multiple directions mein move karta hai (pitch, yaw, roll)
* Joints mein motors strength provide karte hain jabke arm **lightweight** rehti hai.

### **Hands**

* Robot hands **simple grippers** se le kar **complex multi-fingered hands** tak hoti hain:

  * **Anthropomorphic Hands:** 3–5 fingers, har finger mein multiple joints, **power grasps** aur **precision grasps** kar sakti hain. Soft fingertips objects ko securely hold karne mein madad karte hain.
  * **Underactuated Hands:** Kam motors se multiple joints control hote hain, cost aur complexity reduce hoti hai. Mechanical design adaptive grasping allow karta hai.

---

## **Grasping Strategies**

Robots ko **heavy tools** aur **delicate items** dono handle karne hote hain.

### **Power Grasps**

* **Whole hand aur palm** use hoti hai.
* Heavy objects ke liye suitable (e.g., hammer hold karna).

### **Precision Grasps**

* **Fingertips aur thumb** use hoti hain.
* Small ya delicate objects ke liye suitable (e.g., pen uthana).

### **Adaptive Grasping**

* Robots **sensor feedback** se grip adjust karte hain:

  * **Tactile sensors:** Contact pressure detect karte hain
  * **Force sensors:** Grip strength control karte hain
* Objects securely hold hote hain bina damage ke.

---

## **Fine Motor Control**

Fine motor control **precise movements** ke liye important hai.

* **Tactile Feedback:** Fingers pressure aur texture sense karte hain
* **Force Control:** Delicate ya slippery objects ke liye grip strength adjust karte hain
* **Vision-Guided Manipulation:** Cameras objects ko accurately locate aur manipulate karne mein help karte hain

---

## **Tool Use**

Robots humans ki tarah tools use kar sakte hain.

* **Tool Recognition:** Tool aur uske parts identify karna
* **Grasp Planning:** Best way choose karna tool hold karne ke liye
* **Task-Oriented Control:** Tool effectively move karna task complete karne ke liye

---

## **In-Hand Manipulation**

* Robots objects ko **hand ke andar reposition** kar sakte hain bina chhode.
* Techniques shamil hain:

  * **Finger Gaits:** Fingers individually move karte hain object ko roll ya slide karne ke liye
  * **Palm-Finger Synergy:** Palm aur fingers saath use karte hain complex movements ke liye

---

Modern humanoid robots ab **complex, human-like tasks perform** kar sakte hain, jo unhein industrial work aur interactive environments ke liye versatile banata hai.
