# Quickstart: Humanoid Robot Development (Part 8)

Welcome to **Part 8: Humanoid Robot Development**! Is section me, aap seekhenge basics designing, controlling, aur interacting ke humanoid robots ke sath—wo robots jo humans jaise dikhte aur move karte hain. Chahe aap student ho, researcher, ya hobbyist, ye guide aapko essential knowledge dega shuru karne ke liye is exciting field me.

## What You Will Learn

Is part me, aap explore karenge:

* **Humanoid Anatomy**: Seekhein kaise robot bodies humans se inspired hain. Upper aur lower body structure, sensor-equipped heads, aur special joints jo movement allow karte hain ko samjhein.
* **Locomotion and Balance**: Discover karein kaise robots do legs pe walk karte hain. **Center of Mass (CoM)**, **Zero Moment Point (ZMP)** theory, aur walking controllers ke baare me seekhein jo robots ko safe move karne me help karte hain different terrains pe aur actions jaise running ya jumping perform karne me.
* **Manipulation and Dexterity**: Samjhein kaise robot arms aur hands strong aur delicate grasping ke liye design kiye gaye hain. Fine motor control, tool use, aur complex object manipulation ke baare me seekhein.
* **Whole-Body Control**: Explore advanced techniques jo robot ke poore body ko control karte hain. Inverse kinematics, task prioritization, balance maintain karna, aur external forces ke safe response ke baare me seekhein.
* **Natural Human-Robot Interaction (HRI)**: Seekhein kaise robots design karein jo humans ke sath naturally aur safely interact karte hain. Isme human-like appearance aur movements, gestures, eye contact, personal space awareness, aur safety mechanisms shamil hain.
