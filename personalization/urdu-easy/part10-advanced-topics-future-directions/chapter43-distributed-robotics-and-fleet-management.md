# Chapter 43: Distributed Robotics and Fleet Management

This chapter explains how **multiple robots work together**, share tasks, communicate, and manage resources in large deployments like warehouses or rescue missions.

## Key Topics

### Multi-Robot Coordination and Communication Networks

Robots must coordinate and communicate to work as a team.

* **Multi-Robot Coordination**: Robots plan and perform tasks together for faster and better results.
* **Fleet Communication Networks**: Robots share information through Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, 5G, or optical links.
* **Challenges**: Communication must be fast, reliable, and work in changing environments.

### Task Allocation and Load Balancing

Work must be divided properly across robots.

* **Task Allocation**: Tasks are assigned to robots either in advance or in real time.
* **Load Balancing**: Ensures all robots work efficiently without overload.
* **Methods**: Central planners, bidding systems, or robot-to-robot negotiation.

### Swarm Robotics Principles

Inspired by ants and birds, simple rules lead to smart collective behavior.

* **Core Idea**: Each robot follows simple rules and interacts locally.
* **Benefits**: Scalable, fault-tolerant, and adaptable.
* **Applications**: Exploration, sensing, rescue missions, hazardous handling.

### Decentralized Decision-Making

Robots make decisions without a central controller.

* **Concept**: Each robot uses its own sensors and limited communication.
* **Advantages**: No single point of failure, faster reactions, less bandwidth.
* **Challenges**: Ensuring decisions align with team goals.

### Resource Management in Robot Fleets

Shared resources must be used efficiently.

* **Resources**: Charging stations, bandwidth, tools, and workspace.
* **Goal**: Avoid conflicts and ensure availability.
* **Strategies**: Scheduling, dynamic reservations, and priority rules.

This chapter covers how robot teams communicate, divide tasks, and manage shared resources to work effectively in large-scale operations.
