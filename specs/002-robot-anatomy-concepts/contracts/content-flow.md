# Content Flow "Contract" for Chapter 2: Robot Anatomy & Core Concepts

## Purpose
This document defines the logical flow and structure of information presented in Chapter 2 to ensure a clear and progressive learning experience for the reader. It serves as a contract for the content's organization.

## Content Flow Outline

### 1. Introduction to Robot Anatomy
*   **Objective**: Introduce the fundamental components and subsystems of a robot.
*   **Sections**:
    *   Core Components (Sensors, Actuators, End-Effectors, Control Systems).
    *   Robot Subsystems and their interconnections.

### 2. Robot Morphologies and Classification
*   **Objective**: Classify robots based on their physical form and discuss their characteristics.
*   **Sections**:
    *   Mobile Robots (characteristics, types, applications).
    *   Manipulative Robots (characteristics, types, applications).
    *   Humanoid Robots (characteristics, advantages, applications).

### 3. Key Hardware Components
*   **Objective**: Detail the function and types of essential sensor systems and actuators.
*   **Sections**:
    *   Sensor Systems (LiDAR, Cameras, IMUs, Force/Torque Sensors).
    *   Actuators (types, principles).

### 4. Robot Motion and Kinematics
*   **Objective**: Introduce concepts governing robot movement.
*   **Sections**:
    *   Degrees of Freedom (Definition, significance, examples).
    *   Basics of Kinematics (Forward and Inverse Kinematics - conceptual).

### 5. Learning Outcomes
*   **Objective**: Summarize the measurable knowledge goals for the chapter.
*   **Sections**:
    *   List of learning outcomes covering key concepts introduced.

## Validation
*   The content flow MUST align with the learning objectives defined in the `spec.md`.
*   The progression of topics MUST be logical and build upon previously introduced concepts.
*   Each section MUST contribute to the overall understanding of robotics fundamentals.
