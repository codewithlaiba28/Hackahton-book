# Quickstart Guide: Chapter 1 - Foundations of Physical AI

## Chapter Purpose
This chapter serves as the foundational introduction to Physical AI and Embodied Intelligence. Its primary goal is to provide a comprehensive understanding of what Physical AI is, how it evolved, its core guiding principles, and its significance in modern and future technological landscapes.

## How to Get Started

1.  **Understand the Core Definition**: Begin by grasping the fundamental definition of Physical AI and its distinction from traditional AI paradigms.
2.  **Explore the Principles**: Delve into each of the six foundational principles (Embodiment, Sensory Perception, Motor Action, Learning, Autonomy, and Context Sensitivity). Understanding these is crucial for the rest of the course.
3.  **Recognize Key Players**: Pay attention to the role of humanoid robots and why their form factor is particularly important in Physical AI.
4.  **Connect to the Real World**: Review the real-world applications and consider the future prospects and ethical implications of Physical AI.
5.  **Check Your Readiness**: Ensure you meet the stated course prerequisites to maximize your learning experience.

## Learning Outcomes (What you should achieve)

By the end of this chapter, you should be able to:

*   Define Physical AI and Embodied Intelligence.
*   Identify and describe the six foundational principles of Physical AI.
*   Explain the significance of humanoid robots in the context of Physical AI.
*   Discuss current applications, future prospects, and challenges related to Physical AI.
