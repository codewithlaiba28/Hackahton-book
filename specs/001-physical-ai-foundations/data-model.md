# Data Model for Chapter 1: Foundations of Physical AI

## Key Entities and Concepts

### 1. Physical AI
*   **Definition**: Intelligence that emerges from real interactions between a physical body, its environment, and accumulated experience.
*   **Attributes**:
    *   `principles`: Embodiment, Sensory Perception, Motor Action, Learning, Autonomy, Context Sensitivity.
    *   `interaction_type`: Real (physical).
    *   `grounding_mechanism`: Physical body, environment, accumulated experience.
*   **Relationships**:
    *   `distinguishes_from`: Traditional Digital AI.
    *   `exemplified_by`: Embodied Intelligence, Humanoid Robots.

### 2. Embodied Intelligence
*   **Definition**: A concept where intelligence is intrinsically linked to and emerges from physical interaction with an environment through a body.
*   **Attributes**:
    *   `foundations`: Theoretical (e.g., situated cognition), Practical (manifestation in systems).
    *   `evolution_context`: Historical context of AI development.
*   **Relationships**:
    *   `part_of`: Physical AI.

### 3. Humanoid Robots
*   **Definition**: Robots designed with a human-like physical form.
*   **Attributes**:
    *   `form_factor`: Human-like.
    *   `primary_role`: Integration and interaction in human-centered environments.
*   **Relationships**:
    *   `application_of`: Physical AI.
    *   `enables`: Seamless human-robot interaction.

### 4. Environment
*   **Definition**: The physical surroundings or context in which a physical AI agent operates and interacts.
*   **Attributes**:
    *   `type`: Physical.
    *   `interaction_medium`: For sensory perception and motor action.
*   **Relationships**:
    *   `interacts_with`: Physical AI.

### 5. Experience
*   **Definition**: The cumulative result of interactions between a physical AI and its environment, leading to learning and adaptation.
*   **Attributes**:
    *   `source`: Interactions with environment.
    *   `outcome`: Learning, adaptation, accumulated knowledge.
*   **Relationships**:
    *   `informs`: Learning (principle of Physical AI).

### 6. Principles of Physical AI
*   **Categories**: Embodiment, Sensory Perception, Motor Action, Learning, Autonomy, Context Sensitivity.
*   **Definition (per principle)**:
    *   **Embodiment**: The necessity of a physical body for interaction.
    *   **Sensory Perception**: The ability to gather data from the physical world.
    *   **Motor Action**: The ability to act upon the physical world.
    *   **Learning**: The process of acquiring new skills or knowledge from experience.
    *   **Autonomy**: The capacity for self-governance and independent decision-making.
    *   **Context Sensitivity**: The ability to adapt behavior based on the specific circumstances of the environment.
*   **Relationships**:
    *   `constitutes`: Physical AI.

### 7. Applications of Physical AI
*   **Examples**: Robotics in manufacturing, healthcare, logistics, human-robot interaction.
*   **Relationships**:
    *   `demonstrates`: Practical utility of Physical AI.

### 8. Future Prospects of Physical AI
*   **Areas**: Advancements in sensing/actuation, ethical considerations, societal impact, integration with other AI.
*   **Relationships**:
    *   `extends`: Scope and impact of Physical AI.

### 9. Course Prerequisites
*   **Items**: Basic programming skills, Linux familiarity, understanding of sensors.
*   **Relationships**:
    *   `required_for`: Understanding Chapter 1 content.

### 10. Learning Outcomes
*   **Items**: Define Physical AI, identify principles, explain humanoid robot significance, discuss applications/future.
*   **Relationships**:
    *   `achieved_by`: Successful completion of Chapter 1.
