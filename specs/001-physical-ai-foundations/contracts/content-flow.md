# Content Flow "Contract" for Chapter 1: Foundations of Physical AI

## Purpose
This document defines the logical flow and structure of information presented in Chapter 1 to ensure a clear and progressive learning experience for the reader. It serves as a contract for the content's organization.

## Content Flow Outline

### 1. Introduction
*   **Objective**: Define Physical AI and introduce Embodied Intelligence.
*   **Sections**:
    *   Definition of Physical AI (distinction from digital AI).
    *   Historical context and evolution of AI.
    *   Theoretical and practical foundations of Embodied Intelligence.

### 2. Foundational Principles
*   **Objective**: Detail the six core principles of Physical AI.
*   **Sections**:
    *   Individual description of each principle (Embodiment, Sensory Perception, Motor Action, Learning, Autonomy, Context Sensitivity).
    *   Role and significance of Humanoid Robots in Physical AI.

### 3. Applications and Future Prospects
*   **Objective**: Illustrate real-world uses and future potential of Physical AI.
*   **Sections**:
    *   Examples of applications (manufacturing, healthcare, etc.).
    *   Discussion of future trends, challenges, and ethical considerations.

### 4. Course Logistics
*   **Objective**: Inform the reader about prerequisites and expected learning outcomes.
*   **Sections**:
    *   Required skills/knowledge for the course.
    *   Summary of what the reader should be able to do after completing the chapter.

## Validation
*   The content flow MUST align with the learning objectives defined in the `spec.md`.
*   The progression of topics MUST be logical and build upon previously introduced concepts.
*   Each section MUST contribute to the overall understanding of Physical AI fundamentals.
