# Feature Specification: Foundations of Physical AI

**Feature Branch**: `001-physical-ai-foundations`  
**Created**: 2025-11-29  
**Status**: Draft  
**Input**: User description: "Physical AI is defined as intelligence that emerges from real interactions between a physical body, its environment, and accumulated experience. It is grounded in six fundamental principles: embodiment, sensory perception, motor action, learning, autonomy, and context sensitivity. Humanoid robots are particularly important because their human-like form enables seamless integration and interaction within human-centered environments."

## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### User Story 1 - Understanding Physical AI Definition (Priority: P1)

A student reads the introductory section to grasp the fundamental definition of Physical AI.

**Why this priority**: Essential for setting the stage and understanding subsequent concepts.

**Independent Test**: Can be tested by a student recalling or explaining the definition of Physical AI after reading.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a student has no prior knowledge of Physical AI, **When** they read the introductory section, **Then** they can articulate the core definition of Physical AI.

---

### User Story 2 - Identifying Core Principles (Priority: P1)

A student identifies and understands the six foundational principles of Physical AI.

**Why this priority**: Crucial for building a structured understanding of the subject.

**Independent Test**: Can be tested by a student listing and briefly describing each of the six principles.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a student has read the section on principles, **When** asked to list the principles, **Then** they can correctly enumerate embodiment, sensory perception, motor action, learning, autonomy, and context sensitivity.

---

### User Story 3 - Role of Humanoid Robots (Priority: P2)

A student understands why humanoid robots are particularly relevant in Physical AI and human-centered environments.

**Why this priority**: Provides practical context and highlights a key application area.

**Independent Test**: Can be tested by a student explaining the significance of humanoid robots in Physical AI.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a student has read the section on humanoid robots, **When** asked about their importance, **Then** they can explain their role in seamless integration and interaction within human-centered environments.

---

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: The chapter MUST clearly define Physical AI.
- **FR-002**: The chapter MUST enumerate and describe the six foundational principles of Physical AI.
- **FR-003**: The chapter MUST explain the importance of humanoid robots within the context of Physical AI and human-centered environments.
- **FR-004**: The content MUST be accurate and consistent with accepted definitions in the field of Physical AI.
- **FR-005**: The language MUST be accessible to students with the stated prerequisites.

### Dependencies and Assumptions

- **Prerequisite Knowledge**: Students are assumed to have a basic understanding of general AI concepts.
- **Target Audience**: The chapter is aimed at university-level students or professionals new to the field of Physical AI.
- **Content Accuracy**: Content is based on current academic consensus and research in Physical AI.

### Key Entities

- **Physical AI**: Intelligence emerging from real interactions with a physical body and environment.
- **Embodied Intelligence**: A type of AI where intelligence is rooted in physical interactions.
- **Humanoid Robots**: Robots designed with a human-like form.
- **Environment**: The physical surroundings with which Physical AI interacts.
- **Experience**: Accumulated interactions that inform Physical AI's learning.
- **Principles of Physical AI**: Embodiment, Sensory Perception, Motor Action, Learning, Autonomy, Context Sensitivity.

## Success Criteria *(mandatory)*

### Measurable Outcomes

- **SC-001**: After reading, students can correctly define Physical AI with 80% accuracy in a short quiz.
- **SC-002**: Students can list all six foundational principles of Physical AI.
- **SC-003**: Students can explain the role of humanoid robots in Physical AI by providing at least two key reasons.
- **SC-004**: The chapter receives an average clarity rating of 4.0/5.0 from student reviewers.
